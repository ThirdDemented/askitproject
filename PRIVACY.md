# Privacy Policy — The Long Way Home

**Effective date:** September 22, 2026

The Long Way Home is a single-player road-trip/life-simulation game.

## Data we collect
The app does **not** require an account and does not collect, sell, or share personal information.

## Local game data
Game progress and settings are stored locally on the player's device using the app's embedded browser storage. This data is not transmitted to the developer.

## Network use
When an internet connection is available, the game may use public Photon geocoding and OSRM routing services to resolve a city name entered by the player and to request road-distance/turn information for the **cities selected by the player**. The app does not access device GPS, precise device location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, advertising identifiers, or other personal device data.

If routing is unavailable, the game uses an offline distance estimate.

## Analytics, advertising, and purchases
Version 1.0 contains no third-party advertising SDK, analytics SDK, account system, or in-app purchase SDK.

## Children
The Long Way Home contains mature fictional themes including crime, fictional contraband trading, and simulated lottery/scratch-off gambling. It is not designed or marketed for children.

## Changes
If future versions add online accounts, advertising, analytics, purchases, or other data-handling features, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before release.

## Contact
For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support contact listed on the Google Play store page.
