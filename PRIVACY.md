# Privacy Policy — The Long Way Home

Effective September 23, 2026. Publisher: ThirdDemented.

The Long Way Home is a single-player fictional road-trip game. It does not require an account. The developer operates no game-data server and receives no gameplay telemetry. There are no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or purchase SDKs.

## Local progress

Progress, settings and lifetime records are stored in the app's local WebView storage. Starting another life replaces the current journey but keeps lifetime records. Android settings → Apps → The Long Way Home → Storage → Clear storage removes local game data and settings. The app does not offer cloud save or account recovery. Android may include app data in the device's backup service according to the user's system settings.

## Optional online routing

Life setup has a switch for online city search and road routes. When enabled, city names entered in custom search are sent to Photon at photon.komoot.io (provided by komoot), and coordinates for the selected game cities are sent to the OSRM public routing service at router.project-osrm.org. Requests use HTTPS. The services also receive the connection's IP address and ordinary HTTP request information, including a user agent identifying the app. They may retain operational request logs under their own practices. The developer does not control those logs and does not promise that they are immediately erased.

These requests describe cities chosen for a fictional game. The app does not request device location permissions or access GPS, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or advertising identifiers. Do not enter a home address or other sensitive information in the city fields.

Disable online routes to play with built-in cities and offline distance estimates. Custom city search needs an internet connection. A failed online route request falls back to an estimate, and public service availability is not guaranteed.

Provider information: [Photon service and logging](https://github.com/komoot/photon/discussions/539), [OSRM routing service and privacy](https://routing.openstreetmap.de/about.html), [FOSSGIS privacy policy](https://www.fossgis.de/datenschutzerklaerung/).

## Sharing, deletion and children

The developer does not sell data. The optional routing requests transmit the data described above directly to those service operators. Clearing local game data does not remove logs held by them; their privacy contacts handle questions about their logs.

The game includes fictional crime, contraband trading and simulated scratch-offs using only in-game money, and is not directed at children. There is no real-money wagering, chat or user-generated content.

Public support issues are visible to others; do not include private information. Changes to data handling will be reflected in this policy and the store declaration before release.
