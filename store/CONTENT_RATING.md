# Content Rating / IARC Preparation

The final rating is assigned by IARC through Google Play Console. Answer the questionnaire based on the actual content below.

Version 1.0 contains:
- Fictional crime / criminal activity themes
- A hidden fictional contraband trading system
- References to police, confiscation, arrest, and impoundment
- Simulated scratch-off/lottery gambling using only in-game money; no real-money wagering
- Vehicle accidents, breakdowns, fires, and failure/death-style game-over language
- No graphic gore
- No realistic drug-use instructions
- No real-world drug names in the underground market
- No sexual content
- No user-generated content
- No online multiplayer/chat

The game should not be declared as designed for children. The store listing and screenshots should present it as a teen/adult road-life simulation.
