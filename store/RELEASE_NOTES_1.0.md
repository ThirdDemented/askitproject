# Release notes — 1.0.5

The Long Way Home adds a full Trip Computer and persistent lifetime records.

- Live trip odometer, miles remaining, driving time, fuel used, and current cash
- Detailed spending ledger for fuel, repairs, food, motels, supplies, tickets, car purchase, and total spend
- Trading, underground-market, and scratch-off profit/loss tracking
- Vehicle and road statistics including fuel stops, condition, police stops, warnings ignored, and Heat
- Life-on-the-road statistics including shifts worked, possessions sold, motel/car nights, meals skipped, and Marketplace ghosts
- Deadpan “Questionable Achievements” based on how the run actually unfolds
- Persistent lifetime records across runs: total miles, gallons, successful/failed moves, cars bought, total spend, repair/fuel costs, arrests, trading results, and more
- Final trip report available from the ending screen
- Trip Log button available during the drive and lifetime records accessible from the title screen
- Continued high-detail cockpit art, responsive portrait/landscape play, and production Android QA

Same roads. Different you.
