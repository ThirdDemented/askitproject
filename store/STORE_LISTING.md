# Google Play Store Listing

## App name
The Long Way Home

## Short description
Build a life, buy a questionable car, and survive a cross-country road trip.

## Full description
Your life changed. Now you have to get across the country.

**The Long Way Home** is a modern road-life survival game inspired by classic journey games and text-based life simulators. Build your starting life, scrape together enough money to leave, shop for a used car, negotiate with sellers, pack what you can afford, and try to make it to your destination before your money, time, luck, or vehicle runs out.

Every run begins differently.

Choose your age, career, reason for moving, starting city, and destination. Your career changes your income, relocation help, available work, and certain skills. Sell a finite set of belongings, pick up extra shifts, ask family for help, take a bad loan, or gamble a few dollars on scratch-offs.

Then buy a car.

Used cars have hidden problems. Inspect them yourself, pay a mechanic, test drive them, or trust the seller. You get up to three negotiation attempts, and pushing too hard can end the deal.

Once you leave, the game changes.

Drive across the country from inside your vehicle. Manage fuel, fatigue, hunger, cash, vehicle condition, supplies, time, and morale. Deal with motels, diners, truck stops, weather, police, repairs, bad tires, overheating engines, strange roadside encounters, and decisions that may come back hundreds of miles later.

A legal and gray-market trading system lets you buy low and sell high between stops. Curious players may also discover a hidden fictional underground market with higher profits, higher heat, and much worse consequences.

No two trips are meant to tell the same story.

### Features
- Life-sim character setup and multiple reasons for moving
- Career-specific income and relocation assistance
- Finite possessions and limited money-making opportunities
- Used-car listings, inspections, hidden defects, test drives, and negotiation
- Portrait and landscape play, with a cockpit-focused landscape mode
- Road survival: fuel, fatigue, hunger, morale, vehicle condition, time, and cash
- Random events and decision chains
- Legal/gray-market trading and a discoverable fictional underground market
- Original synthesized chiptune music and retro sound effects
- Trip Computer, final trip reports and lifetime records
- Detailed automotive pixel art and animated roadside atmosphere
- Optional online U.S. city search and road routes; offline estimates for built-in cities
- Local save/resume
- No account required
- No ads or analytics

**Same roads. Different you.**
