# Demo feedback — required before release

User playtest feedback, September 23, 2026. The previous visual review does not close these new requirements. Keep publication blocked until implemented and reviewed again.

- Put an actual U.S. map front and center during city selection. Mark origin and destination, show a route following actual roads, and retain search for arbitrary U.S. cities. Clearly distinguish offline estimates from online road routes.
- Preserve the agreed privacy boundary: game-selected city locations only, no GPS or home-address collection, optional online providers with disclosed city/IP transmission, and offline play. The route is for the game, not live navigation.
- Replace road-only motion with a coherent layered driving scene: pavement approaches, roadside power poles and fields pass at appropriate speeds, distant scenery moves more slowly, and the sky/clouds animate subtly. Preserve crisp pixel art, readable controls, reduced motion, and mobile performance.
- Make the dashboard feel active, with moving gauges, appropriate indicator lights and a working radio display.
- Add cockpit interactions: change radio stations, honk the horn, tap the map to see current journey position and remaining distance/time, and tap the dashboard for a readable close-up with odometer and lights and a clear way back.
- Keep a clearly labeled trip timeline visible in the gameplay UI at all times during the journey, including road-event choices. Show the whole trip from starting city to destination, a distinct current-position marker, completed distance and remaining distance. Keep it visible in portrait and landscape without requiring the map or Trip Computer to be opened; tapping it can open the detailed route map. This is an emphasized user requirement, not merely a decorative unlabeled progress bar.
- Make rock/windshield damage visible after the event. Persist the crack across scenes, rotation and reopening until repaired. Consider progressive spreading with clear repair choices; exact progression is an implementation design decision, not yet approved behavior.
- Music is acceptable as a baseline; radio interaction and variety are requested. Use original or appropriately licensed audio.
- User reported refueling followed immediately by another fuel stop. Treat fuel/readout correctness as a release blocker: reproduce full and partial refills, cash-limited refills, repeat event selection, distance/MPG/tank consumption, gauge and numeric display synchronization, and save/resume. Current inspection found that FILL TANK can silently buy only the affordable amount, ADD $20 can leave fuel eligible for another stop, and the price model uses a flat $72 per tank rather than gallons. These are findings to test and clarify, not a confirmed reproduction of the user's exact sequence. Show actual gallons/amount purchased and resulting fuel/range; distinguish optional station availability from urgent need. Ensure other survival readouts reflect their actual saved values.
- User responded positively to regional scenery and early warning cues. Prioritize geography-appropriate scenery and useful engine/dashboard warnings alongside the requested motion work; incidental cabin clutter is secondary polish.
- Fuel affordability disclosure is explicitly required before purchase: state the full refill cost, available cash, affordable purchase amount and gallons, resulting tank percentage and estimated range. When cash cannot fill the tank, label the action as a partial refill rather than FILL TANK; explain if it will not cover the next leg. Confirm the actual purchase afterward. Validate the underlying calculations with controlled tests as well as interactive play; do not rely only on random playthroughs.
- User approved atmospheric traffic plus traffic-jam or pileup/road-closure events. Vehicle animation is optional rather than a requirement for a full live traffic simulation. Match traffic placement and direction to two-lane versus divided-road layouts. Events can offer waiting or a plausible detour with clearly communicated time/fuel tradeoffs; keep crash depiction non-graphic. These are fictional game events, not live traffic feeds.

Implementation inspection confirmed the current windshield event only changes morale or charges for repair; it has no saved glass-damage state. Current driving motion animates a clipped pavement region and a subtle image idle, not separately passing roadside scenery or clouds. These require real implementation changes, not a release-note claim.
