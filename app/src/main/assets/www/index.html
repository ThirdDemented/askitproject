HOW TO PLAY

Choose a life and a destination. Sell what you can spare, earn your travel money, inspect a used car and pack supplies. On the road, keep fuel and condition above zero, rest before you become dangerously tired, and make your deadline. Each choice advances the story. Your journey saves automatically; use RESUME JOURNEY when you return.

Markets have limited stock and demand. Prices change along the route. The Trip Computer records your spending and decisions. Starting a new life replaces the current journey; lifetime records remain.

PRIVACY

Progress, records and settings stay in this app's local storage. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, purchases or developer telemetry. Clearing app storage removes local saves and records.

With online routes enabled, entered city searches are sent to Photon (komoot), and the selected cities' coordinates are sent to OSRM. Those services receive your IP address and normal request information, and may keep operational logs. These are game locations, not your device's GPS location. Online requests use HTTPS. Disable online routes in life setup to use the built-in cities and offline distance estimates. Custom city searches require a connection.

We do not sell data. We cannot erase logs held by routing providers. Read the full privacy policy and contact information.
